# Cancellations and Refunds

## 24-hour refund
When you book directly on the Kenya Airways website for a flight to or from the United States, and the flight is scheduled to depart seven or more days after the booking date, you can cancel within 24 hours of booking for a full refund to your original payment method, with no cancellation fee. To use this, submit the online refund form rather than cancelling from the Manage My Booking tab, and mention the 24-hour policy in the form. These requests are processed within 7 days.

## Requesting a refund
For other refunds, complete and submit the online refund form on the Kenya Airways website. Submitting the form also cancels your full itinerary, or the unused part of it. Whether your ticket is refundable, and how much is refunded, is determined by the rules and conditions of the fare you bought.

## How long a refund takes
Refund requests are processed within a minimum of 21 to 30 business days. A refund to a credit card may be completed faster.
