# Batteries, Power Banks and Electronic Devices

## Spillable and wet batteries
A device containing a spillable battery is not allowed in hand baggage and should be checked in instead. A corrosive wet-cell battery is not permitted in either hand or checked baggage. A security-type case or box that contains lithium batteries or pyrotechnics is also prohibited in both hand and checked baggage.

## Lithium batteries and power banks
Spare lithium batteries and power banks follow international dangerous-goods rules and are generally carried in the cabin rather than in checked baggage. Because the exact watt-hour limits, and the number of spare batteries you may carry, are set by those rules and can change, confirm the current limits for your batteries and power banks with Kenya Airways before travelling.

## Medical devices
Medically necessary needles and syringes are allowed when you carry prescription medication with a professionally printed label. Battery-powered medical equipment is subject to the airline's medical clearance rules.

## Aerosols and lighters
Aerosols such as hair spray, perfume and alcohol-based medicines are limited in hand baggage to 0.5 kg or litre per item, and 2.0 kg or litre in total. For a flight arriving in or departing from the United States, a lighter may not be carried on your person, in hand baggage or in checked baggage.
