# Changing Your Booking

## Changing online
You can manage most of your trip online by logging in to manage your booking on the Kenya Airways website, where you can change flights, choose meals, select seats and add an upgrade.

## Change fees and fare differences
Whether a change is free, carries a fee, or requires you to pay a fare difference depends on the rules of the fare you bought. Lower-priced fares are more restrictive than flexible fares. Because these conditions vary by fare and route, confirm the fee and any fare difference that applies to your ticket before making a change, either online while managing your booking or through the Customer Excellence Center.

## Name changes
A name change on a ticket is treated separately from a date or flight change and is not permitted on all fares. Check the current name-change rules for your ticket with Kenya Airways.

## Getting help with a change
The Customer Excellence Center is open 24 hours and can be reached by phone on +254 734 104 747, mobile +254 711 024 747 or WhatsApp +254 705 474 747 for help changing a booking.
