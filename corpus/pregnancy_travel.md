# Travelling While Pregnant

## Uncomplicated single pregnancy
For a straightforward single pregnancy, a clearance certificate from a Kenya Airways doctor is required once you reach 28 weeks of pregnancy. Kenya Airways does not accept passengers for travel from 36 weeks of pregnancy onward, and any return journey must be completed before you reach 36 weeks.

## Uncomplicated multiple pregnancy
For a multiple pregnancy, such as twins, a clearance certificate from a Kenya Airways doctor is also required from 28 weeks. The cut-off is earlier than for a single pregnancy: passengers are not accepted for travel from 32 weeks onward, and any return journey must be completed before you reach 32 weeks.

## Complicated pregnancy
If the pregnancy is complicated, a clearance certificate from a Kenya Airways doctor is required at any stage, regardless of how many weeks along you are.

## Doctor's letters
A letter from your own doctor does not replace the clearance certificate, but it helps the airline's medical team assess your fitness to fly. For flights to or from the United States, the doctor's letter must be dated within 10 days of departure.
