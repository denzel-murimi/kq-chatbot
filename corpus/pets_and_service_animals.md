# Travelling with Pets and Service Animals

Source: Kenya Airways official website, Pet Carriage page
(https://www.kenya-airways.com/en-ZA/plan/baggage-information/pet-carriage/).
Verify against the live page before travel.

## Pets are not carried in the cabin or the hold

Kenya Airways does not accept live animals as checked baggage in the hold (Animal
in Hold, AVIH) or as pets in the cabin (Pet in Cabin, PETC). Live animals are
transported only as cargo. A passenger wishing to transport a pet should contact
KQ Cargo to arrange shipment; a pet cannot travel in the cabin or as part of a
passenger's checked baggage allowance.

## Service dogs in the cabin

The only live animals accepted in the cabin are trained service dogs. A service
dog must be certified and individually trained to assist a person with a
disability, such as a passenger who is blind or a passenger with a mental or
psychiatric condition. Emotional support dogs are no longer considered service
animals and are not accepted in the cabin.

A service dog is carried free of charge. It does not need a kennel or cage if it
is properly harnessed and does not occupy a seat. The dog sits at the passenger's
feet, or at the feet or floor area of an adjacent unoccupied seat, and must not
block the aisle. Passengers with service dogs may sit in any cabin seat except
emergency exit rows. A dog that behaves disruptively may be refused, even if it
performs assistance tasks.

## Booking and documentation for a service dog

Carriage of a service dog must be reserved in advance using the PNR code "ESAN",
and the information is included in the passenger information list so the crew is
informed. A passenger may be asked to provide a letter from a licensed health
professional, dated within the past year, stating that the dog is necessary for
the passenger's mental health or treatment during the journey or at the
destination.

On flight segments of 8 hours or more, the passenger must either document that the
dog will not need to relieve itself during the flight, or carry the necessary
supplies for the animal; otherwise the dog cannot be accepted.

For health and import documents needed to transport a service dog, passengers
should contact KQ Cargo for guidance, and should check the destination country's
import rules.
