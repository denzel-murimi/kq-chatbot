# Excess, Overweight and Oversized Baggage

## When extra charges apply
If your baggage is more than your free allowance — because you have an extra bag, a bag over the weight limit, or an oversized item — additional charges apply. The exact excess, overweight and oversize rates depend on your route and cabin and change from time to time, so confirm the current charges with Kenya Airways before you travel. Buying extra allowance in advance, where it is offered, is usually cheaper than paying at the airport.

## Oversized and odd-shaped items
A large or odd-shaped item is only accepted if it fits the aircraft used on your route. If an item cannot be carried on any aircraft on your route, it cannot be accepted for the journey. For this reason some special or oversized items must be arranged in advance, and Kenya Airways may decline to carry an item if necessary.

## Packing special baggage
Special baggage must be packed so that the item and its contents can withstand normal handling. When you arrange to carry a special or oversized item in advance, that arrangement is recorded in your booking and checked when you present the item at check-in.

## Sporting equipment and musical instruments
Sporting equipment such as golf clubs, bicycles and surfboards, and musical instruments, are treated as special baggage and may need to be arranged in advance. Because the size limits, handling and any fees for these items depend on the item and the aircraft, confirm how your specific item will be carried, and what it will cost, with Kenya Airways before travelling.
