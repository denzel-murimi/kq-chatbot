# Checked Baggage Allowance

Source: Kenya Airways official website, Baggage Allowance page
(https://www.kenya-airways.com/en/plan/baggage-information/baggage-allowance/).
Allowances apply to Kenya Airways-operated flights and may change depending on
the aircraft operated on the day of travel. Verify against the live page before
travel.

## Free checked baggage by route and class

On most international routes, including Africa to and from America or Europe,
Africa to and from the Middle East or Asia, and flights within Africa, Economy
Class passengers are allowed 2 pieces of checked baggage, each weighing up to 23
kg (50 lbs). Business Class passengers on these routes are allowed 2 pieces, each
weighing up to 32 kg (70 lbs).

On a small number of routes the allowance is 1 piece instead of 2. This applies
to the Bangkok to Guangzhou sector, the Nairobi to Mumbai route, and domestic
flights within Kenya. On these routes Economy passengers are allowed 1 piece up
to 23 kg (50 lbs) and Business passengers 1 piece up to 32 kg (70 lbs). Kenya
Airways domestic points include Kisumu, Mombasa, and Malindi.

## Maximum size per piece

Each piece of checked baggage should not exceed total dimensions of 158 cm (62
inches), measured as length plus width plus height combined.

## Loyalty members

Asante Silver, Gold, and Platinum members are allowed one additional piece of
checked baggage on all routes, matching the cabin of travel: 23 kg for Economy
and 32 kg for Business.

## Infants and reduced mobility

Infants are entitled to one collapsible stroller and one car seat free of
charge, in addition to the free cabin baggage allowance. Incapacitated
passengers may carry one wheelchair or mobility device free of charge. Motorised
wheelchairs and special sporting equipment may be accepted even when they exceed
23 kg, under special conditions or with prior authorisation.

## Excess baggage and baggage pooling

Excess baggage charges apply when a piece exceeds the free weight limit, or when
special baggage such as sporting equipment is checked and a charge is due. For
interline journeys involving more than one airline, excess charges apply through
to the final destination.

Families or groups travelling together may pool their total number of bags, but
they cannot pool weight. For example, where two passengers are each allowed one
23 kg bag, they may not instead check one 18 kg bag and one 26 kg bag, because
the 26 kg bag exceeds the per-piece limit.

## Name labels

Every checked piece must carry a name label, both inside and outside the bag,
showing the passenger's name exactly as it appears on the ticket, a physical
address, and a phone number. An email address is also recommended.

## Special note: Holy Water from Jeddah

Guests travelling from Jeddah are allowed to carry one free bottle of Holy Water,
to a maximum of 5 litres, provided the bottle is sealed to avoid leakage. Kenya
Airways accepts no liability for Holy Water and no claims may be filed for it.

## Codeshare and interline journeys

Where a journey involves codeshare or interline partners, the allowance may
differ and is calculated in compliance with IATA Resolution 302. The free
allowance shown in the Departure Control System or printed on the e-ticket is the
one that applies.
