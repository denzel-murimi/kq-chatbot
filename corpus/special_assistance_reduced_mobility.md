# Special Assistance and Reduced Mobility

## Requesting wheelchair assistance
Wheelchair assistance at the airport is free of charge. You can request it through the Kenya Airways Customer Excellence Center, which is open 24 hours, by phone on +254 734 104 747, by mobile on +254 711 024 747, by WhatsApp on +254 705 474 747, or through the online feedback form.

For flights to or from London Heathrow (LHR), London Gatwick (LGW) and Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG), request wheelchair assistance at least 48 hours before departure. A request made less than 48 hours before departure cannot be guaranteed and depends on airport operations.

## Bringing your own mobility equipment
You may bring your own wheelchair, mobility aid or assistive device free of charge. Reserve the carriage of your equipment at least 48 hours before departure.

A collapsible or dismountable manual wheelchair can be carried in the cabin as hand baggage if it fits the aircraft limits. On the Boeing 787 the limit is 152 x 139 x 149 cm with no maximum weight. On the Boeing 737-300, 737-700 and 737-800 it is 112 x 113 x 85 cm with no maximum weight. On the Embraer 190 it is 120 x 100 x 70 cm with a maximum weight of 125 kg. A wheelchair that does not fit these limits is carried as checked baggage, and you can use an airport wheelchair after check-in.

## Travelling without a companion
You may travel without a companion only if you are self-supporting on board and can assist in your own evacuation. Self-supporting means you can eat, take your own medication, and use the lavatory without help. Crew help with the lavatory is limited to bringing the onboard wheelchair to the lavatory door; the passenger must transfer using their own upper-body strength.

A passenger who is not self-supporting must travel with a personal care attendant who can carry out these tasks. Attendants are generally at least 18 years old and able-bodied.

## Groups
A group of five or more passengers requiring assistance follows the normal group procedure, but any extra equipment and staff must be arranged in advance.
