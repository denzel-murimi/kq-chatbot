# Check-in Times and Deadlines

Source: Kenya Airways official website, Check-in page
(https://www.kenya-airways.com/en/book-manage/post-booking/check-in/). Some
deadlines vary by airport. Verify against the live page before travel.

## Online check-in

For international flights, online check-in opens 48 hours before scheduled
departure and closes 1 hour before takeoff. For flights involving the United
States, online check-in opens 24 hours before departure instead of 48 hours.

For domestic flights within Kenya, passengers are encouraged to check in at least
1 hour and 30 minutes before departure.

## Airport counter check-in

For domestic flights, airport check-in counters close 30 minutes before
departure. Passengers should arrive in good time, as a passenger who misses the
counter deadline may be refused for that flight.

## What you need to check in

To check in online, a passenger needs their booking reference (also called a PNR)
and the last name on the booking. Online check-in lets the passenger select a
seat and obtain a boarding pass in advance, then drop any checked bags at the
baggage drop-off point at the airport.

## United States flights

For flights departing to the United States, additional security requirements
apply and online check-in opens 24 hours before departure. Passengers travelling
to the United States should confirm current requirements before travel.
