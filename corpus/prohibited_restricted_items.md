# Prohibited and Restricted Items

Source: Kenya Airways official website, Baggage Security Guidelines page
(https://www.kenya-airways.com/en/plan/baggage-information/baggage-security-guidelines/).
This is not a complete list of prohibited items. Verify against the live page and
airport security rules before travel.

## Items forbidden entirely

Certain items are not allowed to be carried at all because they are classified as
weapons or Dangerous Goods. Firearms, daggers, flick knives, and other offensive
articles may not be carried in the cabin, except by authorised personnel acting
under the Corporate Security Program.

## Items allowed only in checked baggage

Sharp, pointed, or blunt objects, tools, and toys that imitate such items may only
be carried in checked baggage, not in the cabin. When a passenger declares such an
item at a check-in touch point, staff will ask the passenger to include it in
checked baggage rather than carry it into the cabin.

## Firearms and ammunition

Firearms and ammunition for sporting, law enforcement, or other authorised
purposes are accepted only as checked baggage, and only when the passenger holds
the required export, transit, and import permits. The passenger is responsible for
obtaining these permits. Arms and ammunition are not allowed as cabin baggage
unless authorised by the Head of Security.

Carriage of firearms must be arranged in advance. Handling charges are levied
through an EMD, a special service request (SSR) is required, and the IATA
Dangerous Goods Regulations apply. Customer Service Agents will contact security
personnel or the Station Manager to follow the correct handling procedures, and
local security procedures at the departure and arrival stations must be followed.

## Confiscated items

Any forbidden object detected at security control is confiscated from the
passenger and disposed of.
