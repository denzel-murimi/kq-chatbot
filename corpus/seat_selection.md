# Seat Selection

## When you can choose a seat
You can reserve a seat from the moment you buy your ticket, subject to availability at the time of your request. Some seats may not be available to select, for operational and safety reasons.

## Seat types
Kenya Airways offers standard seat selection as well as paid seat categories such as Preferred seats and Extra Legroom seats, and products including Economy Comfort. The charge depends on the seat type and is set out in the paid-seat terms, so confirm the current price when you select your seat.

## Travelling with an infant or children
An adult travelling with an infant, plus one accompanying passenger on the same booking, is exempt from seat selection charges on regular seats, bassinet seats and the adjacent bulkhead seats, even in a preferred area. If more than one accompanying passenger wants to sit together on Preferred or Extra Legroom seats, charges apply. Children cannot sit in Extra Legroom exit-row seats, for safety reasons. For an unaccompanied minor it is best not to pay for seat selection, because check-in staff assign a suitable seat free of charge.

## Exit-row seats
To sit in an exit row you must be at least 18 years old, able and willing to help in an evacuation, able to read the emergency instructions in English, and able to understand the crew's commands. You cannot sit in an exit row if you are travelling with an infant or children, are a caregiver, use medical equipment, need reduced-mobility assistance, or are travelling with a guide or assistance animal. A passenger who does not meet these criteria may be moved to another seat, without a refund, before or during the flight.

## Refunds on seats
Seat reservations are generally non-refundable, except where an operational, safety or security change forces a seat change. Changing your seat or flight voluntarily is generally not eligible for a refund.
