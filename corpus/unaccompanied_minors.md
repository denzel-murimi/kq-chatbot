# Unaccompanied Minors

## Who the service is for
An unaccompanied minor is a child from 5 up to and including 14 years of age who travels without a parent or adult guardian on the same booking. Kenya Airways requires children in this age range to use the unaccompanied minor service.

Children under 5 years old cannot travel as unaccompanied minors. A child under 5 must always travel with an adult guardian.

Young travellers aged 15 to 17 may also use the unaccompanied minor service if a parent or guardian requests it. For this age group the service is optional rather than required.

## Fees
A service fee applies to unaccompanied minors. The fee is waived for domestic travel within Kenya on routes between Nairobi (NBO), Mombasa (MBA) and Kisumu (KIS). The exact current charge should be confirmed with Kenya Airways when booking.

## How to book
The service is arranged by completing the Unaccompanied Minor Form, which records the child's details, the guardians dropping the child off, and the person designated to collect the child. A dedicated Kenya Airways team handles the booking. Members of the KOOLFLYERS student programme should present their membership card.

## Flights where it is available
The unaccompanied minor service is available only on flights operated by Kenya Airways, not on partner or codeshare flights. Overnight connections are not permitted, and routes with no stops or the fewest connections are preferred. Where a child continues on a domestic flight with another airline on a separate ticket, the service ends at the international airport.

## What the service includes
A staff member assists with early check-in and checks the child's documents. An airline representative then escorts the child through security to the departure gate. During the flight, cabin crew help the child with meals, entertainment and other needs, and a special meal is provided if requested in advance. The parent or guardian and the person collecting the child receive regular updates.

## Collecting the child
The person collecting the child should arrive at the destination airport well ahead of the flight's scheduled arrival time, and bring valid identification matching the details on the Unaccompanied Minor Form.
