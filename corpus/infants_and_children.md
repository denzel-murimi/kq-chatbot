# Travelling with Infants and Children

## Bassinets and bulkhead seats
Many Kenya Airways aircraft have bulkhead seats fitted with bassinets for travelling with a baby. You can request a bassinet when booking or through customer service, and the airline will try to accommodate the request, though a bassinet cannot be guaranteed. A bassinet seat for an adult travelling with an infant is provided free of charge.

## Priority boarding
Families travelling with infants are usually offered priority boarding, and can also choose to pre-board, giving time to settle the child before other passengers board.

## Strollers and pushchairs
Cabin crew can help with storing a stroller during the flight. Strollers are commonly checked at the gate so they are available again on arrival; confirm gate-check arrangements with staff at the airport.

## Baby meals and feeding
Special baby meals are available on request for infants who have started eating solid foods, and should be arranged with Kenya Airways in advance. Lavatories are fitted with changing tables, and cabin crew can warm water for bottles and provide diapers and wipes if you run out.

## Comfort during the flight
To ease ear pressure during take-off and landing, cabin crew can advise on feeding the baby or offering a pacifier. Crew also advise on securing an infant safely during turbulence. Some aircraft offer baby-friendly in-flight entertainment.

## Fares, baggage and age limits
Specific rules cover infant ticketing, the baggage allowance for infants, and the age limits for a lap infant versus a child in a seat. These vary, so confirm the current infant and child rules with Kenya Airways, or on the baggage allowance pages, when booking.
