# Delayed, Lost or Damaged Baggage

## Report before you leave the airport
If your checked baggage does not arrive, or arrives damaged, report it to Kenya Airways baggage services at the arrival airport before you leave the terminal. Reporting straight away starts the trace and is important for any claim you make later.

## Tracking a delayed bag
A delayed bag is traced through the WorldTracer system. You can follow its progress online at worldtracer.aero, using the reference you were given when you reported the bag.

## What to keep
Keep your baggage tags, your boarding pass and the report reference, because these are needed to follow up a delayed, lost or damaged bag.

## Getting help
The Kenya Airways Customer Excellence Center is open 24 hours and can help with baggage problems by phone on +254 734 104 747, mobile +254 711 024 747 or WhatsApp +254 705 474 747.
