# Travel Documents and Visa Requirements

## Your responsibility for documents
Each passenger is responsible for holding the correct travel documents for every country on their journey, including the destination and any country they pass through in transit. Visa requirements can change suddenly and without warning, so confirm your requirements before you travel and make sure your documents are valid. Kenya Airways is not responsible for problems caused by missing or incorrect documentation.

## Electronic Travel Authorisation for Kenya
Travellers to Kenya apply for an Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) through the official government portal at etakenya.go.ke. Apply before you travel, and check the portal for current eligibility, fees and processing times, as these are set by the Kenyan government and not by Kenya Airways.

## Checking requirements for your trip
Entry and transit rules depend on your nationality and your route, so check the passport, visa and health requirements for your specific destination well before departure. If you are unsure, confirm with the relevant embassy or immigration authority, or with Kenya Airways, before you fly.
