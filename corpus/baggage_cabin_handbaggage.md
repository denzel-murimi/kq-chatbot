# Cabin (Hand) Baggage Allowance

Source: Kenya Airways official website, Hand Baggage page
(https://www.kenya-airways.com/en/plan/baggage-information/hand-baggage/).
Airports or other airlines may impose stricter limits. Verify against the live
page before travel.

## Number of cabin bags by class

Business Class passengers may carry 2 cabin bags plus 1 personal accessory item.
Economy Class passengers may carry 1 cabin bag plus 1 personal accessory item.

## Maximum cabin baggage weight

The maximum weight is the combined total of all cabin bags carried. For Business
Class the limit is 18 kg (39 lb) on all routes. For Economy Class the limit is 12
kg (26 lb) on routes to and from the European Union, the United States, and the
United Kingdom, and 10 kg (22 lb) on all other routes.

## Maximum dimensions

Each cabin bag must not exceed 55 x 25 x 35 cm (length x width x height). A
personal accessory item must not exceed 40 x 15 x 30 cm.

## What counts as a personal accessory

The personal accessory item may be a handbag, a backpack, a briefcase, a camera,
or a laptop, each within 40 x 15 x 30 cm. This accessory is allowed in addition
to the cabin bags described above.

## Infants

Infants under 2 years not occupying their own seat may bring 1 cabin bag of up to
12 kg within the standard 55 x 25 x 35 cm dimensions. A car seat for an infant or
small child travelling in a paid seat may be accepted in addition to the normal
allowance.

## Duty-free allowance

Each passenger may bring 1 duty-free shopping bag into the cabin, in addition to
the free cabin baggage allowance.

## Labelling and stricter limits

All cabin bags must carry an "approved KQ cabin luggage" label. Where an airport
or airline imposes stricter cabin limits and a bag does not meet them, the bag
may be checked in free of charge where possible; otherwise normal excess baggage
charges apply.
